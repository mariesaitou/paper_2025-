# Candidate loci for independent SV-like changes at the same genomic region

This document contains the four loci selected for review. The question is whether different salmon lineages carry distinct structural configurations at the same broad Local-PCA locus, consistent with lineage-specific or recurrent SV-like changes. The figures identify candidates; breakpoint-level comparison of the PAF alignments is required for confirmation.

## PCA_SV_ID_0203 — ssa16:33,928,303–73,490,590

**Priority candidate.** The dotplot shows a complex multi-segment architecture with assembly-specific breaks and orientation changes, including differences involving GARN-1 and GARN-2. The region contains 327 overlapping SVs and 32 direction-discordant comparisons. Local-PCA cluster separation is 10.8981. Relate has 1,530 signals and DoLoReS has 4 signals.

- [Dotplot](scenario_figures/dotplot/PCA_SV_ID_0203.png)
- [Haplotype heatmap](scenario_figures/heatmap/PCA_SV_ID_0203.png)
- [Integrated metrics](302_region_integrated_metrics.tsv)

**Please assess:** Do the assembly-specific breaks represent different breakpoint configurations, or do they reflect one older shared structural haplotype with later modifications?

## PCA_SV_ID_0283 — ssa27:10,112,401–16,192,809

**Priority candidate.** European assemblies show mixed or separated alignment arrangements, while North American assemblies show a different predominantly same-orientation configuration. The region contains 49 overlapping SVs and 7 direction-discordant comparisons. Local-PCA cluster separation is 16.8768. Relate has 443 signals and DoLoReS has 1 signal.

- [Dotplot](scenario_figures/dotplot/PCA_SV_ID_0283.png)
- [Haplotype heatmap](scenario_figures/heatmap/PCA_SV_ID_0283.png)
- [Integrated metrics](302_region_integrated_metrics.tsv)

**Please assess:** Are the European and North American configurations compatible with independent structural changes at the same locus, or with different inherited haplotypes containing the same underlying rearrangement?

## PCA_SV_ID_0276 — ssa26:14,630,742–27,656,297

**Priority candidate.** The assemblies share a broad alignment background, with assembly-specific shifted segments, breaks, and alternative orientations. The region contains 125 overlapping SVs and 9 direction-discordant comparisons. Local-PCA cluster separation is 9.0518. Relate has 968 signals and DoLoReS has 1 signal.

## Review outcome

For each locus, please record whether the PAF blocks support: (1) the same breakpoints across assemblies, (2) distinct breakpoints at the same broad locus, or (3) a shared complex structure with uncertain lineage-specific changes. Also note whether the haplotype heatmap and Local-PCA pattern are consistent with the structural interpretation.
